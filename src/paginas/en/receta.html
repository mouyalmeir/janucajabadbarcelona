Cooking with oil

To remember the miracle of the oil, it is traditional on Chanukah to eat fried foods such as latkes (crispy potato pancakes) and sufganiyot (fluffy doughnuts filled with jam or custard).

Rising: 2.5–3 h Frying: 1–2 min per side Can be made parve

Ingredients

Method

  1. Activate the dough. Put the flour in a large bowl, make a well in the centre and add the crumbled yeast, the lukewarm milk and a teaspoon of sugar. Mix gently.
  2. Knead. Add the rest of the sugar, the salt, the eggs and the softened butter. Knead until you have a smooth, elastic, even dough.
  3. First rise. Shape it into a ball, put it in the bowl, cover with a damp cloth and leave it to rest for 1 hour somewhere warm until it doubles in size.
  4. Shape. On a floured surface, roll the dough out to about 8–10 mm thick. Cut out circles with a pastry cutter or a glass about 5–6 cm across. If you’d like ring doughnuts, cut a small 1 cm circle out of the centre of each piece.
  5. Second rise. Place the pieces on floured baking paper, cover them and leave them to rest somewhere warm for 1.5 to 2 hours.
  6. Fry. Heat plenty of oil in a deep frying pan or saucepan (about 3–4 cm deep) over a medium-high heat, without letting it smoke. Fry the sufganiyot in small batches until golden on both sides (about 1–2 minutes per side).
  7. Finish. Lift them onto kitchen paper to drain off the excess oil. Fill them with jam using a piping bag (if you made them whole) and dust generously with icing sugar or caster sugar.
Total: 40 min Makes about 15 Parve

Ingredients

Method

  1. Grate. Peel the potatoes and grate them together with the onion, using a coarse grater or a food processor.
  2. Squeeze dry. Put the mixture in a clean tea towel and wring it out to remove as much liquid as possible. That’s the secret to crispy latkes.
  3. Mix. In a bowl, combine the potato and onion with the eggs, flour, salt and pepper.
  4. Fry. Heat 1 cm of oil in a frying pan. Drop in spoonfuls of the mixture, flatten them slightly and fry for 3–4 minutes per side until nicely golden.
  5. Serve. Drain them on kitchen paper and serve hot with apple sauce or sour cream.
