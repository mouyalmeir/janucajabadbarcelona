The festival ends at nightfall on Saturday 12 December 2026.

Friday 4 and Friday 11: before Shabbat

Light the menorah before the Shabbat candles (before 17:03), using large candles or enough oil to burn at least until 18:30.

Saturday 5: after Shabbat

The second candle is lit from 18:10, when Shabbat ends, after saying Havdalah.

Rosh Chodesh Tevet · Thursday 10 and Friday 11

The month of Tevet begins during Chanukah: Ya’aleh Veyavo is added to the prayers on those days. Saturday 12 is the eighth and last day, known as Zot Chanukah.

Getting home late? If you couldn’t light at the usual time, you can still light later in the night, as long as someone else at home is awake to see the lights.
