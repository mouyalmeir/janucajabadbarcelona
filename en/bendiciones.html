Blessings and songs

On the first night (or the first time you light this year) all three blessings are said. On the other nights, only blessings 1 and 2.

Before lighting

1st blessing · Every night

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱ-לֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו וְצִוָּנוּ לְהַדְלִיק נֵר חֲנֻכָּה.

Baruch atah A-do-nai, E-lo-heinu Melech haolam, asher kideshanu bemitzvotav vetzivanu lehadlik ner Chanukah.

Blessed are You, L-rd our G-d, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to kindle the Chanukah light.

2nd blessing · Every night

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱ-לֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁעָשָׂה נִסִּים לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה.

Baruch atah A-do-nai, E-lo-heinu Melech haolam, she’asah nissim la’avoteinu bayamim hahem bizman hazeh.

Blessed are You, L-rd our G-d, King of the universe, who performed miracles for our forefathers in those days, at this time.

3rd blessing · First night only

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה ה' אֱ-לֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁהֶחֱיָנוּ וְקִיְּמָנוּ וְהִגִּיעָנוּ לַזְּמַן הַזֶּה.

Baruch atah A-do-nai, E-lo-heinu Melech haolam, shehecheyanu vekiyemanu vehigianu lizman hazeh.

Blessed are You, L-rd our G-d, King of the universe, who has granted us life, sustained us and enabled us to reach this time.

After lighting: Hanerot Halalu

While the candles are being lit

הַנֵּרוֹת הַלָּלוּ אָנוּ מַדְלִיקִין עַל הַתְּשׁוּעוֹת וְעַל הַנִּסִּים וְעַל הַנִּפְלָאוֹת, שֶׁעָשִׂיתָ לַאֲבוֹתֵינוּ בַּיָּמִים הָהֵם בַּזְּמַן הַזֶּה, עַל יְדֵי כֹּהֲנֶיךָ הַקְּדוֹשִׁים. וְכָל שְׁמוֹנַת יְמֵי חֲנֻכָּה הַנֵּרוֹת הַלָּלוּ קֹדֶשׁ הֵם, וְאֵין לָנוּ רְשׁוּת לְהִשְׁתַּמֵּשׁ בָּהֶם, אֶלָּא לִרְאוֹתָם בִּלְבָד, כְּדֵי לְהוֹדוֹת וּלְהַלֵּל לְשִׁמְךָ הַגָּדוֹל עַל נִסֶּיךָ וְעַל נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ וְעַל יְשׁוּעָתֶךָ.

Hanerot halalu anu madlikin al hateshuot ve’al hanissim ve’al hanifla’ot, she’asita la’avoteinu bayamim hahem bizman hazeh, al yedei kohanecha hakedoshim. Vechol shemonat yemei Chanukah hanerot halalu kodesh hem, ve’ein lanu reshut lehishtamesh bahem, ela lirotam bilvad, kedei lehodot ulehalel leshimcha hagadol al nissecha ve’al nifle’otecha ve’al yeshuatecha.

We kindle these lights for the salvations, the miracles and the wonders that You performed for our forefathers in those days, at this time, through Your holy priests. Throughout the eight days of Chanukah these lights are sacred; we are not permitted to make use of them, but only to look at them, in order to thank and praise Your great Name for Your miracles, Your wonders and Your salvations.

Psalm 30: Mizmor Shir Chanukat Habayit

Mizmor Shir Chanukat Habayit LeDavid

מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר חֲנֻכַּת הַבַּיִת לְדָוִד.אֲרוֹמִמְךָ ה' כִּי דִלִּיתָנִי, וְלֹא שִׂמַּחְתָּ אֹיְבַי לִי.ה' אֱ-לֹהָי, שִׁוַּעְתִּי אֵלֶיךָ וַתִּרְפָּאֵנִי.ה', הֶעֱלִיתָ מִן שְׁאוֹל נַפְשִׁי, חִיִּיתַנִי מִיָּרְדִי בוֹר.זַמְּרוּ לַה' חֲסִידָיו, וְהוֹדוּ לְזֵכֶר קָדְשׁוֹ.כִּי רֶגַע בְּאַפּוֹ, חַיִּים בִּרְצוֹנוֹ, בָּעֶרֶב יָלִין בֶּכִי וְלַבֹּקֶר רִנָּה.וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי בְשַׁלְוִי, בַּל אֶמּוֹט לְעוֹלָם.ה', בִּרְצוֹנְךָ הֶעֱמַדְתָּה לְהַרְרִי עֹז, הִסְתַּרְתָּ פָנֶיךָ הָיִיתִי נִבְהָל.אֵלֶיךָ ה' אֶקְרָא, וְאֶל אֲ-דֹנָי אֶתְחַנָּן.מַה בֶּצַע בְּדָמִי בְּרִדְתִּי אֶל שָׁחַת, הֲיוֹדְךָ עָפָר, הֲיַגִּיד אֲמִתֶּךָ.שְׁמַע ה' וְחָנֵּנִי, ה' הֱיֵה עֹזֵר לִי.הָפַכְתָּ מִסְפְּדִי לְמָחוֹל לִי, פִּתַּחְתָּ שַׂקִּי וַתְּאַזְּרֵנִי שִׂמְחָה.לְמַעַן יְזַמֶּרְךָ כָבוֹד וְלֹא יִדֹּם, ה' אֱ-לֹהַי לְעוֹלָם אוֹדֶךָּ.

Mizmor shir chanukat habayit leDavid.Aromimcha A-do-nai ki dilitani, velo simachta oyevai li.A-do-nai E-lohai, shivati elecha vatirpa’eni.A-do-nai, he’elita min she’ol nafshi, chiyitani miyardi vor.Zameru lA-do-nai chasidav, vehodu lezecher kodsho.Ki rega be’apo, chayim birtzono, ba’erev yalin bechi velaboker rinah.Va’ani amarti veshalvi, bal emot le’olam.A-do-nai, birtzoncha he’emadta leharerei oz, histarta fanecha hayiti nivhal.Elecha A-do-nai ekra, ve’el A-do-nai etchanan.Mah betza bedami berideti el shachat, hayodcha afar, hayagid amitecha.Shema A-do-nai vechoneni, A-do-nai heyeh ozer li.Hafachta mispedi lemachol li, pitachta saki vate’azreni simchah.Lema’an yezamercha chavod velo yidom, A-do-nai E-lohai le’olam odeka.

A psalm.A song for the dedication of the House, by David.I will exalt You, L-rd, for You have raised me up and have not let my enemies rejoice over me.L-rd my G-d, I cried out to You and You healed me.L-rd, You brought my soul up from the depths; You kept me alive so that I would not go down to the pit.Sing to the L-rd, you His pious ones, and give thanks to the memory of His holiness.For His anger lasts but a moment, and His favour a lifetime; in the evening there is weeping, and in the morning, joy.I said in my tranquillity: “I shall never be shaken.”L-rd, by Your favour You made me stand firm as a mighty mountain; You hid Your face and I was dismayed.To You, L-rd, I call; to my L-rd I plead: “What gain is there in my blood if I go down to the grave?Will the dust praise You?Will it proclaim Your truth?”Hear, L-rd, and be gracious to me; L-rd, be my help.You turned my mourning into dancing; You removed my sackcloth and girded me with joy, so that my soul may sing to You and not be silent.L-rd my G-d, I will thank You for ever.

Traditional song: Ma’oz Tzur

First verse

מָעוֹז צוּר יְשׁוּעָתִי, לְךָ נָאֶה לְשַׁבֵּחַ,
תִּכּוֹן בֵּית תְּפִלָּתִי, וְשָׁם תּוֹדָה נְזַבֵּחַ.
לְעֵת תָּכִין מַטְבֵּחַ, מִצָּר הַמְנַבֵּחַ,
אָז אֶגְמוֹר בְּשִׁיר מִזְמוֹר, חֲנֻכַּת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ.

Ma’oz tzur yeshuati, lecha na’eh leshabe’ach,
tikon beit tefilati, vesham todah nezabe’ach.
Le’et tachin matbe’ach mitzar hamnabe’ach,
az egmor beshir mizmor chanukat hamizbe’ach.

Stronghold, Rock of my salvation, it is fitting to praise You. Restore my House of prayer, and there we will offer You thanks. When You silence the enemy who torments us, I will complete with song and psalm the dedication of the altar.

Other prayers of the festival

Throughout the eight days of Chanukah the full Hallel is said in the morning service, and V’Al HaNissim is added to the daily prayers and to Grace after Meals.
